# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

PalmWatch is for oil-palm estate owners, plantation managers, agronomists, field teams, and research partners evaluating or operating an evidence-led monitoring program.

## Product Purpose

PalmWatch turns repeat aerial surveys, field observations, and confirmation data into a traceable view of palm health. The homepage must make the research approach credible, show how estate-scale signals become field actions, and give prospective partners a clear route into the study scope.

## Brand Personality

Evidence-led, calm, premium. PalmWatch should feel technically capable and field-grounded without presenting screening as diagnosis or using speculative claims.

## Anti-references

- Scroll hijacking or motion that fights the browser.
- Unsupported yield, accuracy, disease, or return-on-investment claims.
- Fabricated testimonials, contact details, and social accounts.
- Generic SaaS dashboards, decorative glass effects, or excessive animation.
- Clinical interfaces that lose the physical plantation and field-work context.

## Design Principles

- Show the estate before showing the interface.
- Make every claim traceable to evidence or clearly label it as a study target.
- Let motion explain the observation-to-action sequence.
- Keep the path from plantation overview to field verification obvious.
- Adapt the composition to the viewport instead of merely shrinking it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard access. Preserve readable type at 200% zoom, 44px touch targets, visible focus states, semantic landmarks, informative alternative text, and a complete reduced-motion presentation.
